Instructions for Evaluators

Welcome to the SLM (Summarization Language Model) human evaluation task. Your feedback will help improve summarization quality for Indic languages.

Please carefully read the original text and the provided summaries, then evaluate each summary according to the criteria below:

  • Fluency: How grammatically correct and readable is the summary?
  • Coherence: How well-organized and logically connected is the summary?
  • Relevance: How well does the summary capture the key points of the original text?
  • Adequacy: How well does the summary convey the main message without missing critical information?
  • Overall Quality: Your general assessment of the summary's quality.

Rate each aspect on a 1-5 scale where: 1=Poor, 2=Fair, 3=Good, 4=Very Good, 5=Excellent
